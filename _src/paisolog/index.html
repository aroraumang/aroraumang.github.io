Everything you need, nothing you don't

  • Every account, any currency

    Cash, bank accounts and credit cards, with totals converted to your main currency.

  • Receipts with every entry

    Attach photos and PDFs to a transaction. They're kept with it, on your phone.

  • Credit card bills, sorted

    Billing cycles, statements and due dates, with a reminder before a bill is due.

  • Budgets that keep up

    Weekly, monthly, yearly or custom budgets, by category and account.

  • Recurring, handled

    Rent, salary and subscriptions are added for you, right on time.

  • Reports and search

    See where your money went each month, and find any entry by note, payee, tag or amount.

Private by design

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start logging.

  • No servers, no tracking

    No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. Your entries aren't sent anywhere.

  • Locked to you

    Turn on Device Lock to open the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode.

  • One small exception

    To convert currencies, the app downloads exchange rates. That request includes only your base currency.
