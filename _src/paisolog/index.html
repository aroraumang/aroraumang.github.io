PaisoLog

A private expense tracker for iPhone and Android.

Everything you enter is stored on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no PaisoLog servers. The only thing the app downloads is daily exchange rates.

What it does

  • Track expenses, income and transfers across cash, bank accounts and credit cards, in any currency.
  • Keep receipt photos and PDFs with each transaction.
  • Follow credit card bills and due dates.
  • Set budgets and see where your money goes each month.
  • Add rent, salary and subscriptions automatically.
  • Back up to a file you control, with an optional password.
  • Lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode.
