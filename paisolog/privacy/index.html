PaisoLog Privacy Policy

PaisoLog is a personal expense tracker. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no PaisoLog servers. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your data stays on your device

Transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, notes, tags and attachments are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share this data.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data unless you made a backup.

Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, the app downloads exchange rates from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API). The request contains only your base currency code. Like any internet request, it also reveals your device's IP address to that service. No financial data is sent.

You can use the app fully offline. Conversions then use the last downloaded rates.

Attachments, camera and photos

If you attach receipts, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to add one. Attached files are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.

Backups and exports

Backups and CSV exports that you create are saved to a location you choose. You can protect backups with a password. Where the file goes after that (for example a cloud drive you pick) is up to you and that service's own privacy policy.

The app also keeps a few automatic backups in its private storage on your phone: one each week and one before every restore or reset. They stay there like the rest of your data and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Device lock

If you turn on Device Lock, your phone's operating system handles Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode checks. PaisoLog never sees or stores your biometric data.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone (card bills, budgets, a daily reminder). They are created on the device; there is no push server and nothing about them is sent anywhere. Bill amounts appear in notifications only if you allow it.

Error log

When something goes wrong, the app records the error message and technical details in a log on your device (the last 50 entries). It contains no transactions or account details. The log is never sent automatically. You can view, share or clear it in Settings, and it is shared only if you choose to send it to us.

Children

PaisoLog is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an updated effective date and shown in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email paisolog@umangarora.com.
