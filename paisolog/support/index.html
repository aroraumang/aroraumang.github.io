Reporting a problem

These details help a lot:

  • What you were doing, and what you expected to happen.
  • What happened instead, and whether it happens every time.
  • The steps to make it happen again, if you know them.
  • Your app version. You'll find it in Settings > About & support > Version.
  • Your phone model and its iOS or Android version. You can find these in your phone's Settings app under General > About (iPhone) or About phone (Android).
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can. Feel free to cover any amounts or names you'd rather not share.

Please don't send your backup file or its password unless we ask for it.

Sharing the error log

When something goes wrong, PaisoLog notes the error on your phone. This log has technical details only, with no transactions or account details, and it never leaves your phone unless you share it.

  1. Open Settings and scroll to About & support.
  2. Tap Error log. It shows how many errors have been recorded.
  3. Tap Share and send it to paisolog@umangarora.com with your email app.

If it says no errors have been recorded, just describe the problem in your email. You can also tap Clear at any time to delete the log.

Backups and restoring

Your data lives only on your phone, so backups are how you keep it safe and move it to a new phone. You'll find everything under Settings > Storage & backup.

Making a backup

Create local backup saves a single .paisolog file with all your data and receipt attachments to a folder you choose. You can add a password to protect it.

  • Keep a copy somewhere other than your phone, such as a cloud drive or your computer, so it's safe if your phone is lost.

Remember your backup password. There is no way to open the file without it, and we can't recover it for you.

Automatic copies on your phone

PaisoLog also keeps a few copies inside the app: one each week, and one just before every restore or reset. You can see them in Backups on this phone. They are handy for undoing a mistake, but they are deleted if you uninstall the app or lose your phone, so they don't replace a backup you save yourself.

Restoring

  1. Go to Settings > Storage & backup > Restore from backup.
  2. Pick your .paisolog file, and enter its password if it has one.

Restoring replaces everything currently in the app with what's in the backup. PaisoLog saves a copy of your current data first, and you can undo the restore if you picked the wrong file.

Moving to a new phone

  1. On your old phone, create a backup and save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone.
  2. Install PaisoLog on the new phone.
  3. Restore the backup as described above.

Uninstalling PaisoLog deletes all its data, so make a backup first.

Privacy
